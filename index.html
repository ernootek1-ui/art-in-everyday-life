01 / THE BIG IDEA

Art is a part of
being human.

Art is a way of expressing ideas, emotions, beliefs, and experiences. It can be something we create, something we use, or something we see around us.

Look beyond frames and galleries. The design of a chair, a colorful meal, a song, or a carefully decorated room can all be forms of art.
